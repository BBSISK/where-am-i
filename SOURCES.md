## Crossings (rivers, canals, protected areas)
A crossing is announced only when the straight line between two consecutive GPS fixes actually intersects the river line or the site/canal boundary (`src/services/crossings.ts`, unit-tested). Driving alongside a river is not a crossing. Leaving a protected area is never announced; a site is announced at most once per 30 minutes; nothing plays over speech already in progress, and nothing is queued. GPS jumps longer than 400 m (tunnels, lost signal) are ignored. Built by `pipeline/` (see [pipeline/README.md](pipeline/README.md)).

## Not available yet
- Whole-name meanings. Logainm's explanatory notes are **not** available to us (see decision log, 2026-11-04 Gaois reply). The app only links to the place's logainm.ie page; it never speaks, quotes, rephrases or summarises a note.

## Removed (November 2026)
All earlier curated heritage content (folklore, census, archive, art, military and archaeological claims) was removed: much of it could not be traced to primary sources, and some citations and quotations were found to be wrong or invented.

## Editing rules for spoken heritage text (towns without story files)
Monument and building threads use the official record's own words. Monuments to Visit text additionally drops the standard access disclaimer appended to every entry ("Please note that many of these monuments may be on private land…"). The only edits (see `pipeline/heritage.py`, all unit-tested) are: removing the "Compiled by / Date of upload" footer; removing parenthetical bibliographic references, record codes and measurements; "c. 1780" → "circa 1780"; compass shorthand → words. Each thread links to its official record.

## Story narration (summaries in our own words), from 2026-11-05
Towns with a reviewed file in `pipeline/review/stories/` are narrated as stories: a ~30-second teaser and ~1-minute "Tell me more" stories, **written in our own words from the cited source records only**. CC BY 4.0 allows adaptation with attribution; DIB and Dúchas (CC BY-NC 4.0) allow it non-commercially. Logainm explanatory notes are never used, in any form.
- **References are shown on screen, never spoken**: each story lists "Sources (adapted, not quoted)" with the record ids.
- **Fact check (build fails the story otherwise):** every number, year, ordinal and proper name in a story must appear in the text of the records it cites (`pipeline/stories.py`, tested).
- **Freshness:** each story stores a hash of every record it was written from; if a record changes, the story is not spoken until it is re-checked.
- **Hedges kept:** where a record says "probably" or "according to", the story keeps that uncertainty; where records disagree, the story says so ("records differ…").
- **Status:** "draft" until Barry signs the town off; the audit page (`/audit.html`) shows each story beside the source text it was written from.

## When sources disagree (conflict rule)
Official sources sometimes disagree. Example: the SMR record says Maynooth Castle was erected "probably in the late-1180s"; the Monuments to Visit guide says it was "founded in the early 13th century", with the keep built c. 1203. The app never merges or reconciles such texts: each source is spoken as its own passage, introduced by its own attribution, and both record ids are kept on the thread. Tested in `pipeline/test_heritage.py` and `pipeline/test_area_data.py`.

## Images
At most one image per item, only from an exact record join, always with credit, licence note and a link to the record. No image is better than a guessed one. Images are linked from the publisher, not copied into this repo.

## Licensing status (decision log)
- **2026-11-04: non-commercial for now (Barry).** Dúchas (National Folklore Collection) is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0, which forbids commercial use. The app may use Dúchas material while it is non-commercial. **Before any commercial use**, either obtain permission from the National Folklore Collection (UCD) / Gaois, or remove Dúchas content (it will sit behind a single build switch). All other sources listed above are CC BY 4.0 or ODbL and allow commercial use with attribution.
- **2026-11-04: NIAH photographs (`niah-images`) used under the non-commercial decision.** The NIAH dataset (text) is CC BY 4.0, but the photographs it links to are covered by the buildingsofireland.ie terms: educational or private use with credit to the NIAH; commercial use only with prior written agreement. They sit behind `NON_COMMERCIAL_SOURCES` in `pipeline/build_areas.py`. **Before any commercial use**, get written agreement from the NIAH or set the switch to False.
- **2026-11-04: Dictionary of Irish Biography (`dib`) is CC BY-NC 4.0** (entry pages: "licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution Non Commercial 4.0 International license"). An earlier note in our planning said CC BY 4.0; that was wrong. Used under the non-commercial decision, behind `NON_COMMERCIAL_SOURCES`. dib.ie asks crawlers to wait 30 s between requests and not to use /search; `fetch_dib.py` reaches each entry by its DOI, 30 s apart.
- **2026-11-04: Gaois reply on Logainm explanatory notes (Diarmaid, Gaois).** "Logainm data" (the placenames as made available through the API) is CC BY 4.0; required attribution: "Irish-language placename data by Logainm © Government of Ireland and licensed under CC BY 4.0" (with links), now used in the app footer. The explanatory notes are authored by Placenames Branch researchers (© Government of Ireland), are outside the CC BY licence, are not in the API, and **must not be reproduced, rephrased, or used to create derivative works**; any reference to them needs standard academic citation. Consequence: no whole-name meanings from Logainm notes, ever (including summaries or "in our own words"). The glossary elements we use come from the API and are Logainm data under CC BY 4.0.
